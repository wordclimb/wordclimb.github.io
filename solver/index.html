How word ladders work

A word ladder starts at one word and ends at another. Each step changes exactly one letter, and every step has to be a real word: COLD → CORD → WORD → WARD → WARM. Lewis Carroll invented them in 1877 and called them Doublets. The fewest steps a ladder can be done in is called par.
