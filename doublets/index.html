Lewis Carroll's Doublets

Before word ladders had a name, Lewis Carroll made them up. He came up with the idea around Christmas 1877 and published the puzzles in Vanity Fair in 1879. He called each puzzle a Doublet.

The rule is the one Word Climb still uses: change one letter at a time, and every step has to be a real word. Below are some of his classic challenges. Try them in your head, then tap to see one way up.

"Par" is the fewest moves it can be done in with the Word Climb dictionary. The answers shown use everyday words, so some take a few more moves than par. Can you match it?
