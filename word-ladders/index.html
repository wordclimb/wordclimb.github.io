Free printable word ladder puzzles

Start at the bottom word and climb to the word at the top. Change one letter on each rung, and every rung has to be a real word. Par is the fewest moves it can be done in.

Free to print and share, including in classrooms. The answers are at the bottom, on their own page when printed.

Answers

Most puzzles have more than one way up. Any climb that reaches the top in par counts.

  4. RIB → ROB → ROW → HOW
